What the pass measured on the shipped pane

graphs render outside their canvas

Every populated stage: 9 of 9 stations laid out beyond their map's box (077's map is 392px tall; its stations sit at y≈1060–1675, two at x=−24). A fitView-vs-computed-height regression 004 introduced — the plan predicted it, no scenario asserted containment.

one screen per epic, mostly empty

Six epics stack vertically, each with its own landing rail and its own copy of the edge legend (×3). The master–detail above replaces the stack: one graph, one rail, one legend.

text collisions on the Desk

Measured overlapping pairs on every epic row: "COMPLETED · epic-002" × "dispatch", "implementer" × "us1 · paged". The milestone bar needs collision-free label slots, not smaller fonts.

hard 1216px cap

Desk content is centred at 1216px regardless of viewport: 672px of dead margin per side at 2560. The frame above is fluid to 96rem.

stale attention forever

The fixture floor's escalation and question are pinned to a 22 Aug capture instant, so the Desk leads with two expired countdowns permanently. Expired items should collapse to one line each under a "stale" fold.

status is scattered

Spec state lives in the rail of nowhere, node state on cards, landing state on a separate rail. The seven-stop ladder above unifies them per story — the thing this mock exists to show.
